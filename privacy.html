Privacy policy

Effective October 11, 2026.

Healthbot Email Intake is a personal desktop utility. It accesses Gmail account identity, message metadata, message contents, labels and attachments to identify and preserve selected self-addressed healthbot messages, and to move successfully preserved messages to Trash.

OAuth credentials are kept in owner-only files on the account owner's computer, outside the project Git repository. Message captures and intake status are stored in the owner's local project. The owner may sync selected captures to their private GitHub repository or review them with their existing AI assistant. The automated hourly poll does not call AI services or research websites.

The utility does not sell data, use it for advertising, send email, permanently delete mail, or publish message content. Access is restricted by its account and message-selection checks. Its Google permission is broader than the operations it implements.

The owner controls retention of local captures and private backups. Revoking access in Google Account connections stops future Gmail access; deleting retained captures and backups is a separate owner action. Gmail Trash is governed by Google's retention rules.

Support contact information is available on the app's Google consent screen. This policy will be updated if the utility's data handling changes.
