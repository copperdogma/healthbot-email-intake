Healthbot Email Intake

A personal desktop utility that saves selected, self-addressed Gmail messages into a local research inbox. It checks hourly while the computer and user session are available.

The utility selects inbox messages sent by the account owner to themselves with the word healthbot in the subject. It saves message content and attachments locally before moving that exact message to Gmail Trash. It does not permanently delete messages or send mail.

Google Gmail modify permission is required for reading messages and moving them to Trash. The utility verifies the configured account before operating. Authorization can be revoked in your Google Account connections settings.

This utility is for personal use. Support contact information is available on its Google consent screen.
